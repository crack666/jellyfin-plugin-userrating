User Rating

Personal rating (UserData.Rating) in the stock web UI: sort, cover badge (hover or always-on via the star in the library toolbar), set the score on the detail page. IMDb CSV import on this page. No fork of jellyfin-web. github.com/crack666/jellyfin-plugin-userrating

Native clients

Android, Android TV, FireTV, webOS and Chromecast know nothing about a personal rating — Jellyfin offers them neither a field nor a sort option for it. Each switch below hands your score to one field those clients do render, so they can show it and sort by it. Responses are rewritten on the way out; nothing is written to the database, and switching a toggle off brings the original values back on the next request. Titles you have not rated report no value at all, rather than the original one.

Replaces CriticRating — the tomato icon, on a 0–100 scale, so a 9 shows as 90. Rarely filled in, so little is lost. Discreet: many clients show it on the detail page only.
Replaces CommunityRating — the stars, already on a 0–10 scale, so your score shows unchanged. This is the prominent rating on detail pages and on cover art, which makes it far more visible than the critic field — at the price of not seeing what the community thought while it is on.

IMDb import

IMDb → Your Ratings → Export. Required columns: Const (tt…) and Your Rating. Match on ProviderIds.Imdb. Only the personal score is written; play state is left alone.
